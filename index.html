About

I'm a master's student at Boston University working on geometric computer vision, multimodal representation learning, and geometry-aware generative modeling. I'm particularly interested in how mathematical structure and probabilistic modeling can inform the design of better visual models.

I currently work with Prof. Boqing Gong at Boston University, where my research spans representation learning, generative modeling, and visual perception.

Selected
Projects

Few-Step Generative Modeling on Riemannian Manifolds

Ongoing

Investigating whether few-step generative models on SO(3) preserve multimodal rotation distributions while reducing sampling cost. Comparing Riemannian Flow Matching and Riemannian Consistency Models through mode coverage, distribution fidelity, and sampling-step tradeoffs.

VLA Perception Stabilization

PyTorch · OpenVLA · LIBERO

Studied temporal perception stability in vision-language-action models. Designed object-centric temporal stabilization using Kalman filtering and structured state representations, with evaluations under perturbations.

Clownfish Tracking & Multiview Perception

SAM 2/3 · YOLO · OpenCV

Developed underwater-video preprocessing and multiview diagnostics for tracking and annotation. Investigated lens distortion, triangulation, and cross-view reprojection consistency.

Multimodal Emotion Recognition

CLIP · DINOv2 · wav2vec

Developed emotion classification using visual, audio, and text representations, with late-fusion and confidence-weighted ensembles achieving 80% macro-F1.
