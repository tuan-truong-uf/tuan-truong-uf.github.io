About

My research interests lie at the intersection of mathematical structure and visual learning. I'm particularly interested in how geometry and probability can inform the design of generative models and learned representations.

I currently work with Prof. Boqing Gong at Boston University. My recent work spans generative modeling on rotation manifolds, representation evaluation on infant-centric video, and multiview perception.

Research

ONGOING · GENERATIVE MODELING

Few-Step Generative Modeling on Riemannian Manifolds

Investigating whether few-step generative models on SO(3) preserve multimodal rotation distributions while reducing sampling cost. Comparing Riemannian Flow Matching and Riemannian Consistency Models through mode coverage, distributional fidelity, and sampling-step tradeoffs.

SO(3) Flow Matching Consistency Models
REPRESENTATION LEARNING

Learning from Infant-Centric Video

Evaluating representations learned from egocentric developmental data through linear probing and downstream classification. Studying what spatial and visual information is retained by pretrained vision encoders.

Contrastive Learning Multimodal Learning Evaluation
COMPUTER VISION · MULTIVIEW GEOMETRY

Clownfish Tracking and Multiview Perception

Developing underwater-video preprocessing and cross-camera reprojection diagnostics to support tracking and annotation. Investigating geometric consistency, lens distortion, and multiview validation.

Tracking Multiview Geometry Video

Selected Projects

VISION-LANGUAGE-ACTION

VLA Perception Stabilization

Studied temporal perception stability in vision-language-action models using OpenVLA and LIBERO. Designed object-centric temporal stabilization and evaluation pipelines for perception drift under perturbations.

PyTorch OpenVLA LIBERO
MULTIMODAL PERCEPTION

Multimodal Emotion Recognition

Developed multimodal emotion classification using visual, audio, and text representations. Evaluated late-fusion and confidence-weighted ensembles across seven emotion categories, achieving 80% macro-F1.
